The Rosstat leaks (rosstat-leaks.com): the Russian death register and the SMEV broadcast of death records, deaths to 29.09.2026. My count: 497,396 war deaths, men of 18-64 (65 for court decisions), counted by a broad filter (a military cause, military employment, death notices from abroad, court decisions on the missing, no-cause deaths in combat areas, the Rostov military registry offices), each act and each person once. Home region: the residence in the act or the broadcast; where there is none, the registry office’s usual municipality, the birthplace or the region. Registry office: the region of the office that registered the death. Place of death: where the act says the man died, matched to a region; deaths in the rest of Ukraine are off this map. Projected: my estimate of 776,671 dead to October 2026 (the registered plus GSUA’s reports scaled to the register for the deaths still to be registered), each month’s missing spread over the regions in that month’s registered mix. Rayons and settlements: the 215,848 deaths whose home town was matched by name (rayons also take municipality centres). Rayon and oblast borders: geoBoundaries 2017. Male population: russia_per100k_map.html. Salary (2025) and signing payments: gogov.ru, via russia_payments_map.html.
